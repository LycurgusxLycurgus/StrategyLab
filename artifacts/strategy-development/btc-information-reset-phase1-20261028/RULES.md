# Frozen research candidate: low funding state, 24-hour long

This is one new systematic candidate, `low_percentile_funding_long_24h_v1`, evaluated on two execution grids. It is a source-informed creation, not replication of a published trading strategy. No economics have been run. The signal, risk and controls below are root decisions frozen before any future comparison; implementation belongs to a later authorized Phase 2.

## Signal and availability

Use the existing checksum-verified Binance USD-M BTCUSDT funding events, preserving actual millisecond timestamps and numerical rates. At event time `t`, let `W` contain only events whose timestamps lie in `[t - 180 days, t)`. Require full input coverage over those 180 days and at least 300 prior events. Calculate `(count(W < current_rate) + 0.5 * count(W == current_rate)) / len(W)`. A percentile at or below 0.10 creates a long intent; implement this boundary exactly as `5 * (2 * less_count + equal_count) <= prior_count`. Do not impose an additional negative-rate condition: a relative low state may still have positive funding. Ties use exact stored numerical rates. No short signal, price-impulse filter, trend filter or discretionary confirmation applies.

Use a common 187-day warmup from the dataset start for the candidate and both controls. The extra seven days lets the stale-information control use the same complete feature history; it is not a selected profitability parameter. Features may use earlier authorized history in later annual folds, but positions and queued intents cannot transfer across folds.

Historical settlement timestamps are not measured receive timestamps. For this research model, assume the finalized rate is usable only after the first complete 5m or 15m bar ending strictly after `t + 1ms`. Decide at that bar end plus 1ms and fill at the first execution-grid open strictly after that decision. An 08:00:00.006 settlement therefore permits modeled entry at 08:10 on 5m and 08:30 on 15m. Preserve the raw timestamp; do not round it back to the settlement boundary for availability. This conservative delay is an assumption to challenge, not a latency guarantee. The intent expires at that single scheduled fill; do not retry it on a later bar.

## Position and risk

Trade only the linear BTCUSDT perpetual, long only, with USDT cash collateral. Start every annual account with 100,000. At entry, target notional is 10% of current flat-account equity, including entry fees within that 10% budget: `quantity = floor_to_step(min(0.10 * equity / (slipped_entry * (1 + fee)), 120 BTC))`. Use quantity step/minimum 0.001 BTC and minimum notional 50; reject an illegal size. Fixed quantity after fill, one position, no pyramiding, no same-open replacement and no overlapping entry orders. Suppress an intent when occupied at its decision time; record that suppression distinctly and apply it identically to the stale control. Also reject an entry when a position is carried into its fill open, even when the old position exits at that open.

Exit by market order exactly 24 hours after actual entry. There is no target, trailing rule, hard stop, forced same-day flattening or Friday close. Low gross notional supplies the initial risk limit; it does not guarantee a maximum realized loss or reproduce the previous 0.25% stop-risk sizing. The old stop-distance cost-admission rule is inapplicable to a model without a stop. Do not silently add a stop or extend the hold to improve results. All overnight, weekend, funding and price-gap exposure remains part of the experiment.

Use adverse slippage at each market fill, both-leg fees, fixed quantity and cash/equity accounting. Credit/debit signed historical funding only while the position is open at the actual event timestamp; a carried position owns exact-open funding before a coincident time exit. A position entered after a settlement receives none of that already-paid settlement. Future rates may change sign. Retain existing API settlement marks and verified five-minute mark-open proxies with a 1% adverse adjustment to funding cash flows: subtract 1% of the absolute nominal payment. Proxies remain approximations. Mark equity and adverse equity every execution bar using hypothetical adverse-slipped liquidation prices and their exit fees: the long close mark is `close * (1 - slip)` and adverse mark is `low * (1 - slip)`. These valuations do not create actual exit trades. Record collateral/solvency failures; do not fabricate a normal exit for unsupported liquidation behavior. This is a low-notional research account, not a validated live margin adapter.

For adverse intrabar equity, take the lower valuation using cash both before and after the bar's owned funding events against the adverse price mark. A funding credit must not mask a price low that may have preceded it. Exact-open funding and deterministic time exits retain their known ordering. Ordinary close equity includes all events strictly before the bar's end.

If cash becomes negative, marked/adverse equity becomes nonpositive, a funding obligation cannot be represented, or the collateral/venue contract is otherwise unsupported, preserve the failure trace and mark that context `INVALID_EXECUTION`; it cannot qualify. Do not silently continue a bankrupt account or substitute a hypothetical liquidation fill. Any future live margin model remains a separate acceptance obligation.

## Controls

`stale_funding_7d_long_24h_control_v1` uses the same opportunity timestamps, entry delays, size, occupancy and 24-hour exit. At current funding opportunity slot `j`, use the already-computed classification from actual event slot `j - 21`, seven days earlier. An 8h slot identifies the schedule only; availability still uses actual timestamps. Verify one actual event in each required slot. No current-rate classification participates in that control's entry decision. A stale low state creates its intent; otherwise stay flat. This tests freshness descriptively, not a strict null: funding regimes can persist for a week, exposures differ, and the control can retain genuine information.

`continuous_long_control_v1` opens once at the first funding opportunity's legal fill after the shared warmup in each fold, independently of any funding classification, using the same 10% fee-inclusive entry budget and quantity constraints, then holds its fixed quantity through the fold. Mark the unclosed position at cutoff with estimated exit slippage and fee; do not invent a terminal fill. Charge every owned funding event. Report it as a benchmark, never a selectable strategy. Cash earns zero and needs no market replay.

Report net PnL per notional-day as well as ordinary account return. The denominator sums `abs(quantity held after opening events) * bar.open * bar_duration_days` across every bar. The numerator is ending marked equity minus starting equity, including all costs and estimated exit fee on an open position. Multiply the ratio by 10,000 for bps per notional-day. This is a descriptive exposure-efficiency comparison, not a return achievable by retrospectively resizing the benchmark.

## Chronology, costs and gates

Use five separate annual folds: 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and January–August 2026, with flat initial state. The first fold's common warmup ends in July 2022. Funding event, observation and legal fill must belong to the fold; fill must occur strictly before its end. Positions whose 24-hour exit falls beyond cutoff remain censored and marked; do not fetch later prices or force an early exit. All historical folds are exposed research evidence. Protected September 2026 onward and the existing prospective final windows remain untouched.

The previous November-only warmup is insufficient for this new 187-day shared history requirement. If the previously reserved December 1, 2026 final evaluation start were retained, necessary funding history would begin May 28. That includes protected intervening dates. Do not access them now or silently change their designation. A later, separately authorized frozen-finalist contract must resolve the warmup permission or a genuinely fresh evaluation schedule before a final test. This is an unresolved future final-validation dependency, not evidence that such a test is ready today.

Freeze three cost conditions, each with 6bps fee per fill: base 2bps slippage per fill, standard stress 4bps, severe sensitivity 20bps. Round-trip fee-plus-slippage is approximately 16, 20 and 52bps before funding. Standard stress is the mandatory promotion screen for this new candidate. Severe sensitivity is reported separately, not silently omitted or used for parameter selection. This choice is made before any new returns are viewed: the earlier 52bps mandatory gate belonged to a tail-price-shock strategy; it is not evidence that every delayed funding-state entry incurs that cost. The weaker source headroom at 52bps is a real limitation. If subsequent execution evidence requires higher costs, those costs must become mandatory and affected economics must be re-earned. Do not reinterpret earlier failed campaigns under these new gates.

The planned complete comparison is 90 annual evaluations: three roles × two execution grids × five folds × three costs. There are two candidate contexts and four controls, all long only. One fixed 24-hour horizon, no parameter searches, no mutations and no short or 48/72-hour rescue trials.

A candidate context may advance only if every condition holds:

1. All semantic, input, timing, funding, sizing and execution checks pass, with the complete saved matrix and identities.
2. Base marked return is positive in both 2025 and 2026; at least 30 closed trades in each and 100 combined; pooled five-fold net closed-trade profit factor is defined and at least 1.05; every fold's close-equity drawdown is at most 10%.
3. Sum of 2025/2026 standard-stress account returns is positive.
4. Recent summed base return strictly exceeds the same-grid stale-information control; pooled recent base net PnL per notional-day strictly exceeds the same-grid continuous-long benchmark.
5. Recent closed net PnL remains positive after removing the entire best contributing entry week. Assign trades by entry to Monday 00:00 UTC weeks. Report clustering, event counts, overlapping evidence and annual concentration; counts are not independent samples.

Rank qualifying contexts by greatest worst-fold base return, then greatest mean-fold base return, then lexicographic context ID. Controls are never winners. No pass means return to research without changing thresholds, costs, hold, directions or controls. A pass would authorize only an exact-context Phase 3 acceptance discussion, not training, live orders or a profitability guarantee. The present Phase 1 instruction does not authorize running this comparison.
