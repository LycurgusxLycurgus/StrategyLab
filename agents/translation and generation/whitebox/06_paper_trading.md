# Phase 6 — Paper trading and graduation

Produce an evidence-backed forward graduation decision for the exact frozen finalist; do not submit live orders.

## Operating contract

Read `AGENTS.md`, `agents/translation and generation/agents/codex_phase_operator.md`, relevant architecture and unresolved-proof entries before acting. Schedule: `systematic_v2`; runtime workflow selectors remain unchanged. Work within authorized scope/budget, preserve immutable evidence, and report missing prerequisites honestly. The operator's shared report/evidence contract applies. Use neutral requirements, not illustrative strategies, assets, parameter values or trading rules.

## Inputs

Require Phase 5 accepted finalist/final holdout, qualifying asset/timeframe/direction scope, full comparison-to-finalist lineage/trial ledger, exact target build, risk limits, deployment venue and actual paper/shadow adapter. Paper evaluation must not silently expand beyond the qualified scope. Prompts do not supply a universal broker connector. If the required adapter is absent, report BLOCKED and the concrete implementation prerequisite.

## Task

1. Freeze paper protocol before starting: environment/venue, code/model/settings versions, data clock, latency deadlines, capital/cost model, position limits, coverage by relevant sessions/regimes/setups and economic/operational pass/fail criteria. Duration and sample coverage must follow predeclared contextual evidence needs, not fixed universal defaults.
2. Distinguish shadow hypothetical fills from venue paper execution. Record their fill/liquidity/impact limitations; neither proves real-money execution. Verify permissions, credentials handling, reconnects, stale data, duplicates, rejected orders, sizing and protective exits without placing real orders.
3. Reconcile timestamped reference signals, submitted paper orders, acknowledgements, fills, positions, equity and costs. Investigate missing/duplicate/out-of-order events and deviations. Test kill switch, maximum loss/exposure controls, restart/recovery and reconciliation before accepting operations.
4. Compare forward evidence to the frozen contract, including fees/slippage/funding/borrow and infrastructure costs where applicable, latency and drawdown. Preserve outages and rejected trades. Insufficient coverage remains pending/BLOCKED rather than a statistical pass.
5. A rule/parameter/execution change creates a new version and reopens impacted phases; do not tune on paper observations and keep calling the same stream independent. Preserve original candidate outcome and versioned replacement evidence.
6. If evidence meets all gates, prepare a separate live-review packet including venue feasibility, capital-at-risk limits, monitoring, rollback and required approvals. Do not activate real trading or infer authorization from graduation.

## Output and validation

Deliver reconciliation evidence tied to the qualified context and immutable candidate lineage, and exactly GRADUATED_FOR_LIVE_REVIEW, NOT_GRADUATED, or BLOCKED, with unmet obligations and next owner. Graduation is eligibility for a separately authorized live review, not live permission or a profit guarantee. Return correctness failures to their owning phase; economic failure returns to research only as a new documented development campaign.

Include exact artifacts, reproducible commands, source/code/settings/data identities, separate correctness/fidelity/execution/economic/target verdicts, controls, uncertainties, budget and next action. A later phase never compensates for failed earlier proof.
