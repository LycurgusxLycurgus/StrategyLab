# Phase 1 — Research and candidate selection

Produce a research-only batch of at least three fresh, distinct, complete mechanical hypotheses for the requested market and execution context. Research readiness is not a validated signal, backtest result or permission to execute Phase 2.

## Operating contract

Read `AGENTS.md`, `agents/translation and generation/agents/codex_phase_operator.md`, relevant architecture and unresolved-proof entries before acting. Schedule: `systematic_v2`; runtime workflow selectors remain unchanged. Work within authorized scope/budget, preserve immutable evidence, and report missing prerequisites honestly. The operator's shared report/evidence contract applies. Use neutral requirements, not illustrative strategies, assets, parameter values or trading rules.

## Inputs

Accept the requested strategy or methodology, asset set, market, execution timeframe and separate holding horizon, direction choices, execution/risk constraints, available data, sources, prior failure/component ledger and explicit research/testing authorization scope. Preserve explicit user choices. When timeframes or directions are unspecified, research and justify the most pertinent choices, then freeze a bounded comparison matrix before testing.

## Task

1. First determine whether the requested approach is already inherently systematic/mechanical or still requires discretionary judgment. State the evidence and unresolved subjective decisions. Keep this assessment separate from historical origin/operation metadata; a discretionary-origin formulation may already be completely mechanical.
2. For an already systematic approach, research the strongest available complete mechanical approaches for the requested context. Investigate original sources, existing research and inspectable implementations. Evaluate repository suggestions and familiar strategies on the same evidence criteria as alternatives; familiarity or prior mention grants no selection priority.
3. For a discretionary approach, perform two ordered research sub-steps:
   - Investigate the strongest ways to transform it into a systematic approach. Establish what each method actually formalizes, which concepts it preserves, and what it omits or changes. Distinguish an indicator or partial detector from a complete trading strategy.
   - Evaluate the strongest complete mechanical formulations. Label each as a supported faithful translation, translation of only supported parts, or deliberately designed systematic emulation of selected concepts. Record fidelity evidence and limitations separately from correctness against the new specification. Every trading decision must be rule-based with zero discretion remaining. Never silently substitute an arbitrary quantitative proxy for an undefined source concept.
4. Build the operator's research-quality dossier for every candidate: observable/measurement validity, mechanism and competing explanations, source-to-target instrument/data/clock/horizon/execution changes, replication/selection exposure and credible captured-effect-versus-cost rationale. Reject unsupported proxies or inaccessible/future inputs rather than replacing them for convenience. A disclosed different observable is a new hypothesis. Paper correlation or gross return is not net edge; stop/target distance is not expected captured movement. Prespecify a bounded component test with suitable controls, independent-event coverage and uncertainty, without running it in research-only mode. Freeze evidence-based shortlist criteria before selection: source quality and traceability, implementation completeness, causal testability, relevance to requested market/horizon/execution constraints, data feasibility, credible net-cost evidence, limitations and distinctness. Separate independently established evidence from author/vendor claims. Preserve URLs, dates, exact versions, licenses and attribution; disclose mismatched contexts and absent evidence.
5. Research at least three fresh hypotheses on every attempt; target 3–4 test-ready candidates within budget and explain selection over rejected alternatives. “Top” means best-supported to investigate, not proven profitable. After a Phase 2 failure, use its actual failure attribution and retained components to choose new mechanisms, meaningful revisions or a justified mix. A revision must have a new identity, explicit parent/change, diagnosed failure, distinct causal prediction and falsification test; minor parameter/filter changes and duplicate contexts do not count as fresh strategies. At least three complete evidence-supported candidates are required for PASS_TO_PHASE_2. If fewer qualify, remain INCOMPLETE/BLOCKED, explain the gap and continue only within approved research budget; never pad a batch or pass a single candidate plus controls.
6. Before testing, provide each candidate's complete causal rule specification: inputs/state and information availability, signal timing, entries, exits, invalidation, position management, sizing/risk and execution assumptions. Record source-to-rule interpretation, formalization and deliberate adaptations with rationale. Identify every unresolved implementation decision; resolve decisions affecting trading behavior and justify designed additions before candidate testing. Unsupported source claims remain explicit; a missing behavioral rule is a blocker, not license for an implicit default.
7. Declare the candidate-by-asset-by-timeframe-by-direction matrix, research/comparison budgets, common data/cost/risk contract, chronological development/validation partitions and genuinely untouched final holdout. Define evaluation criteria, absolute advancement requirements, controls and stopping/return conditions before results. Keep candidate selection distinct from subsequent parameter tuning. Freeze component endpoints/controls and full-strategy advancement gates separately; track hypothesis families, revisions, contexts, controls, folds/costs/replays and independent events separately.

## Output and validation

Deliver the classification, systematic-source investigation or both discretionary research sub-steps, at least three supported fresh candidate dossiers and complete rule specifications, rejected alternatives/gaps, meaningful-revision lineage, retained component evidence, resolved decision log and frozen diagnostic/comparison protocol/matrix/budget. Explain why the batch is new, revised or mixed and what each member tests that failed work did not.

PASS_TO_PHASE_2 requires at least three supported complete fresh candidates, no unresolved behavior-affecting decisions, feasible data/execution and preregistered diagnostic/comparison/advancement rules. Fewer candidates or missing evidence means INCOMPLETE/BLOCKED, not a completed batch. Feed Phase 2 failures into the next research questions without hiding prior trials.

The user-facing handoff must explicitly state: Phase 1 was research/specification only; no new candidate trades, signal stress tests or economic comparisons ran; none is validated or promoted; Phase 2 is the real initial testing/stress comparison that determines Phase 3 promotion or return to research. Request approval of this batch and bounded Phase 2 protocol before running it. If the user already explicitly approved research plus testing of this new bounded batch, record that authorization, announce the transition and execute Phase 2 rather than stopping at readiness. Research-only approval or an old failed batch's approval does not authorize it.

Include exact artifacts, reproducible commands, source/code/settings/data identities, separate correctness/fidelity/execution/economic/target verdicts, controls, uncertainties, budget and next action. A later phase never compensates for failed earlier proof.
