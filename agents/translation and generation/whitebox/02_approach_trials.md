# Phase 2 — Controlled comparison

Implement and initially stress-test the approved batch of at least three distinct complete candidates from Phase 1. Produce separate measurement, signal-quality and full-strategy economic verdicts; decide Phase 3 promotion or a documented return to research.

## Operating contract

Read `AGENTS.md`, `agents/translation and generation/agents/codex_phase_operator.md`, relevant architecture and unresolved-proof entries before acting. Schedule: `systematic_v2`; runtime workflow selectors remain unchanged. Work within authorized scope/budget, preserve immutable evidence, and report missing prerequisites honestly. The operator's shared report/evidence contract applies. Use neutral requirements, not illustrative strategies, assets, parameter values or trading rules.

## Inputs

Require explicit human approval of this batch's bounded Phase 2 testing scope, at least three supported fresh complete candidate dossiers, Phase 1 classification/research record, causal rules/resolved implementation decisions, source/adaptation and meaningful-revision provenance, retained component evidence, data identities and frozen diagnostic/comparison protocol. Research readiness alone is not authorization. The protocol must declare evaluation criteria and absolute advancement requirements before results, a bounded candidate-by-asset-by-timeframe-by-direction matrix, chronological development/validation partitions, an untouched final window and trial budgets. “Ensemble” means controlled candidate comparison, not variants of a preselected winner or automatic signal combination.

## Task

1. Preserve requested timeframe and direction choices. If either is unspecified, choose and justify the most pertinent settings from the research before testing. Freeze the actual matrix, per-context comparisons, aggregate selection policy, controls and total evaluation budget before examining outcomes. Disclose any infeasible or omitted cells; do not silently narrow the user's request or present untested contexts as supported.
2. Implement each candidate against its complete accepted rule specification, preserving exact source/license/provenance and candidate identity. Build necessary indicators first, but verify the complete strategy. Resolve behavior-affecting ambiguity before running it; justified additions change the specification and must be documented, not hidden in code.
3. Independently validate positive/negative cases, source/measurement construct validity, state and information availability, original/normalized data, repainting, look-ahead bias and realistic submission/fill/position/cost behavior before trusting performance. Unsupported proxies, future/inaccessible inputs, engine behavior or semantic defects block the relevant claim/conclusion; they are not evidence of an economic loss or universal noise. Use the operator's INVALID_PROXY / INVALID_MEASUREMENT disposition until resolved.
4. Execute the preregistered bounded component/discrimination screen on development data before expensive full comparison where it can reject unsupported formulations. Measure relevant outcomes from legal information/entry times, dependence-aware uncertainty, independent events and time/context stability; compare defensible controls matched for side, clock/regime, exposure, turnover and availability as applicable, plus prespecified ablations/placebos. Lower losses from reduced participation are not automatically information value. Classify NOISE_OR_NO_USEFUL_EFFECT, INCONCLUSIVE, PROMISING_COMPONENT or SUPPORTED_SIGNAL under the operator's evidence definitions. Preserve scope/uncertainty; underpowered tests are not proof of noise. Count these screens as research exposure; no final holdout is available to them.
5. Run fixed, unoptimized candidates that satisfy the declared screen under comparable data, costs, capital/risk constraints and chronological validation. Apply one protocol with justified context-specific execution assumptions; disclose differences that limit comparability. Keep strategy selection separate from parameter tuning. Phase 4 owns parameter search by default; any indispensable Phase 2 tuning requires separate baseline-backed authorization, a predeclared bounded nested-development budget and full disclosure, never inspection of reserved holdouts.
6. Compare candidates against the predeclared absolute gates and relevant controls, not only relative rank. Assess net economics, risk, sample uncertainty, stability across the tested contexts, concentration and implementation completeness/complexity. Record all candidate/context runs, rejected implementations, manual changes, tuning and repeated trials in the cumulative ledger. Treat reused evaluation windows as development evidence; preserve the final holdout for the frozen Phase 5 finalist. Separate predictive association, entry-delay decay, strategy exit/occupancy conversion and costs using raw-to-net/decision-to-fill diagnostics. Saved-fill attribution is not a cost-free counterfactual replay; signal support and full-strategy economics remain separate.
7. Identify which candidates and contexts actually satisfy every advancement requirement. Select an eligible candidate under the frozen selection policy only if such evidence exists. Never declare a winner merely because it lost less than others or because later optimization might rescue it. A combination or behavioral change is a new hypothesis requiring updated specification and comparison; separate component successes do not prove it.

## Output and validation

Deliver the complete planned/executed comparison matrix and justified screening stops, implementations and causal/execution proof, measurement/signal/economic verdicts, raw-to-net attribution, controls/uncertainty and independently meaningful event counts. Retain promising/supported components with definitions/versions, contexts, effects, evidence limits, failed wrapper and next falsifiable question; do not discard them merely because full strategies failed. They remain research components, not Phase 3 entrants. Preserve all trials, contexts and failed attempts.

PASS_TO_PHASE_3 requires a candidate meeting all predeclared requirements with current proof. Hand off its exact accepted rules/code/settings, qualifying asset/timeframe/direction scope, provenance, comparison evidence, limitations, consumed development windows, protected holdouts and remaining budget. If none qualifies, RETURN_TO_PHASE_1 with failure attribution for every candidate, retained components and precise questions the next research must resolve. Stop the current comparison. Tell the user that the next research attempt will prepare at least three fresh candidates and that they will be tested only after approval of the new batch/protocol. Recommend whether new mechanisms, meaningful revisions or a mix best address the evidence; any not-yet-researched batch is provisional. Explicitly explain that Phase 1 is research-only by default and Phase 2 will perform the real initial testing/stress comparison for Phase 3 or another return. Do not silently start a new batch, issue only a bare return verdict, or ask redundant approval if the user already explicitly authorized that new bounded research-and-test cycle. Missing data/capability/proof means BLOCKED; invalid tests require repair rather than a fabricated strategy verdict.

Include exact artifacts, reproducible commands, source/code/settings/data identities, separate correctness/fidelity/execution/economic/target verdicts, controls, uncertainties, budget and next action. A later phase never compensates for failed earlier proof.
