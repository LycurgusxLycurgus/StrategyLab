# Strategy roads and box terminology

Active development follows the six-phase systematic_v2 operator. Whitebox and blackbox describe policy inspectability, not successive maturity stages.

An explicit mechanical strategy can originate from discretionary ideas. Research transformation methods, then evaluate complete mechanical formulations with explicit faithful/partial-translation or designed-emulation status. Preserve interpretation/formalization/adaptation provenance and evaluate the complete rule-based hypothesis. Do not claim exact source judgment was preserved merely because a backtest passes.

A transparent indicator plus opaque decision model is a mixed architecture. A frozen learned policy may operate systematically; discretionary origin, systematic deployment and policy transparency are separate facts. Record origin, operation, governance, interpretability, data type and learning mode separately. Existing runtime metadata names are unchanged.

The active road is research and distinct complete candidate selection -> controlled comparison -> qualifying base -> optimization -> unified mutation/optimization -> paper graduation. “Ensemble” means comparing the supported shortlist, not minor variants of an assumed winner. Every Phase 1 attempt is research-only by default and needs at least three fresh test-ready hypotheses; incomplete evidence never licenses padding. Meaningful revisions need new causal predictions. Approval of the new batch/protocol precedes Phase 2; explicit combined approval permits a visible same-workflow transition. Preserve the predeclared context matrix, absolute advancement gates and trial accounting; if none qualifies, return specific evidence, retained components and a justified next-batch proposal for at least three candidates, with approval needed before testing. Combining signals requires a separately tested hypothesis. No mandatory blackbox phase follows a whitebox strategy.

The future discretionary-origin road is causal indicator -> candidate event -> decision model -> deterministic risk gateway -> shadow/paper. Model and social/ML work is **plan only**, not installed or authorized by these phase prompts. See [the future roadmap](../plans/discretionary_and_blackbox_roadmap.md) for CLEF assessment, matched controls, latency/versioning, point-in-time data and learned-policy safeguards.

Source fidelity, executable-reference correctness, execution, economics and target parity remain separate verdicts for every road. Measurement validity, incremental signal evidence and full-strategy economics are also separate: unsupported proxies are vetoed, insufficient evidence is not pure noise, and supported components may earn research priority but never bypass a failed strategy's gates. Policy opacity does not waive validation; model explanations do not establish mechanism transparency.
