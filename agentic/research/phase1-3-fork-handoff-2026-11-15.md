## Compare nine ways to research strategies

A **lane** is the provenance of an input; a **method** is the process that generates and falsifies hypotheses; a **family** is the proposed market behavior. Tag all three. Different input sources or models can independently restate the same idea; that counts once.

| Method | What to do and what would decide it | Main limitation and cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Inspectable-script replication | Select documented, license-compatible established scripts. Port the original rules, prove signal equivalence at valid timestamps, then test whether the exact implementation survives target costs. | Fast when code is legible; popularity is not evidence. Veto inaccessible source, undocumented repainting, or incompatible licensing. |
| 2. Closed-book model ideation | Isolate a tool-less task with the brief only; generate mechanisms and complete rules from model knowledge. Independently verify data feasibility and proposed effect against a strong simple baseline. | Cheap and diverse in wording, not necessarily mechanism. Claims and references are unverified until checked; veto unverifiable predicates. |
| 3. Primary-effect replication | Extract a measurable claim from a primary study or authoritative technical source. Reproduce a minimal effect where feasible, then test instrument, availability, horizon and cost transfer one change at a time. | Higher reading/data cost. If replication inputs are missing, label the test an adaptation; a gross source association supplies no target net return. |
| 4. Mechanism-first original design | Start from a plausible market constraint and specify observable events, competing explanations, direction, horizon and a rejection test before wrapping the signal in trading rules. | Can yield genuinely distinct candidates; sparse events or unavailable point-in-time fields can block it. |
| 5. Discretionary rule extraction | Translate authorized rulebooks, annotated cases or practitioner notes into a checklist and state machine. Use independent positive, negative and boundary cases to check fidelity. If behavior changes, label supported partial translation or designed emulation. Then test incremental value net of costs. | Ambiguity can multiply variants. If a required judgment cannot be observed or justified, stop; don't invent a proxy. |
| 6. Failure-driven component rescue | Use prior ledgers to isolate a promising component and its specific failed wrapper. Form a new prediction about what should improve and run a parent/child ablation under matched exposure and execution. | Efficient use of negative evidence; veto unchanged mechanisms masquerading as retries or an inversion with no independent rationale. |
| 7. Transport ladder | Begin from credible evidence in another context. Map what must remain invariant and change market, information timing, horizon or execution in controlled steps. Test the weakest transfer link first. | Reveals where the effect dies, but multiple steps consume budget; veto unsupported transfer without pretending the source effect was disproved. |
| 8. Falsifier-first competing hypotheses | Start with the closest rival explanation from prior evidence. Generate paired deterministic hypotheses that predict different outcomes on the same eligible events; preregister a control or placebo that separates them. | More design effort, fewer costly ambiguous tests. Veto non-discriminating controls and post-outcome changes. |
| 9. Execution-first design | Bound attainable fill, turnover, latency and break-even movement before searching for a mechanism at a compatible horizon. Test conditional effect at the legal entry against conservative fill and delay stress. | Cuts candidates that cannot cover friction. Cheap execution is not a signal, and hypothetical favorable fills don't count. |

Available lanes can include consolidated open-source scripts; closed-book model hypotheses; primary research; user-provided narratives or rules; inspectable practitioner casebooks; licensed point-in-time market/event facts and venue specifications; validated or failed internal components; and original mechanism design. Review access, license, provenance, temporal availability, independence from the protected data and a concrete contribution. Closed-book work may request later verification but must not pretend it browsed. Keep numbers and example strategies out of the canonical prompts.

## Choose the research method on evidence, before editing prompts

The source-lane tournament is the **proposed default to challenge**, not an observed winner. Compare it with single-method baselines (including the existing research workflow) and a quality-first challenger that assigns effort by evidence instead of enforcing source coverage. Evaluate all nine methods in a bounded first pass on the same real briefs, then deepen only shortlisted methods under a separately approved budget. Use distinct real development and unseen evaluation briefs covering systematic creation, faithful versus partial discretionary translation, known duplicates, and missing/proxy inputs. Include an expected `INCOMPLETE` case so plausible filler cannot win. Keep protected market data/outcomes out; use only the authorized brief and eligible evidence; record what prior outcomes each method legitimately used.

Freeze time/token/source-access budgets, output cap and scoring before looking at output. Supply equal opportunity, not artificially identical input access: method-specific access is part of the treatment; record costs and do not conceal an inaccessible lane. Score anonymized rule cards against an independently frozen rubric, then separately verify provenance/licensing. Hard-fail hidden discretion, unavailable timestamps, counterfeit fidelity, unsupported proxies and duplicate-as-novel claims. Compare supported distinct test-ready cards per unit of effort, control/falsifier quality, veto detection, correct abstention and coverage by brief type. Preserve each individual output, sources, model/tool versions and cost; do not infer profitability from rubric scores. If authorized, apply the same Phase 2 gates to equal-budget admitted shortlists to compare useful yield, and report no clear winner if evidence cannot distinguish methods. Modify a phase prompt only after a real-input baseline vs. proposed run shows improvement on unseen briefs without losing existing gates, and after separate approval.

## Source-lane variant process

- **Round 0 — distinct candidates.** One proposal slot per eligible lane, with no guaranteed admission. Freeze the actual cap; accept one defensible distinct strategy per slot, deduplicate and retain all rejects. Use at least three fresh supported candidates for formal testing or report `INCOMPLETE/BLOCKED`. Do not fill quotas with close cousins, directions/timeframes of the same rule, placebo controls or lightly rewritten prior failures.
- **Rounds 1 and 2 — structural descendants only.** For each qualifying parent, give each still eligible lane at most one child proposal. Freeze parent/child caps, diagnostic and full-test budgets, and selection rules before outcomes. Eight eligible lanes can yield eight first proposals; three eligible parents make up to 24 children; four eligible parents in the next round make up to 32. These are ceilings, not entitlements. Compare children with the immutable parent. Each child needs a new identity, a material causal change justified by a diagnosed limitation or supported component, a new prediction and test, and a new approval. Threshold swaps are tuning and belong outside this search. Retain a qualifying original parent if the children do not beat it under the same comparison policy.
- If no full candidate qualifies, stop the round: no survivor multiplication. Retain supported components without calling them strategies. A fresh or mixed batch needs a failure-specific plan and approval; after the two variant rounds or budget exhaustion, stop and report the best supported result or no qualifier. Don't silently restart until profitable.

## Run Phases 1–3 and preserve what decided them

**Phase 1 — research only by default.** Each card records ID and parent/delta, provenance and exact licensed source, fidelity category, observable versus proxy, mechanism and rival, input/time/units, warmup and missing-data behavior, data availability, causal transitions, exact entry/exit/invalidation/sizing/position/order rules, latency and fills, locked settings, independent event capacity, cost headroom, provenance gaps and falsifier. Define behavior for identical input and tie cases; no human or LLM discretion at trade time. Freeze common comparison and advance criteria before outcomes, with justified context-specific differences. State plainly that specs are not tested trades. An explicit bounded combined authorization lets you proceed into Phase 2 with a visible transition; otherwise request approval.

**Phase 2 — screen, then stress.** Verify causal implementation and exact data identities before P&L; optional Pine parity only if required by the actual workflow, not a research prerequisite. Check semantic positive/negative cases, availability versus event time, replay, fill realism and missing data. Use a narrow signal screen and matched controls where relevant to direction, time/regime, exposure and turnover. Veto invalid proxies; distinguish adequate evidence of no useful effect from weak or inconclusive evidence. Preregister sample adequacy and dependence/overlap-aware uncertainty, meaningful incremental effect, net expectancy/PF, downside and drawdown limits, concentration and chronology/robustness, and net sensitivity to realistic adverse costs. Choose thresholds by context before outcomes. Don't assume a usable effective-trial estimate from correlated historical runs; disclose dependence and use an appropriate predeclared selection treatment or independent confirmation. Preserve absolute gates even if every candidate fails. Any exploratory screen or prior selection exposure belongs in the ledger; all research tests use authorized development windows. Reserve the final untouched holdout for its later gate.

**Phase 3 — consolidate a qualifier only.** Freeze the exact qualifying rules, context/code/data/parameters, default execution, approved controls and source mapping. Reuse supported engines or validate the minimum needed engine with independently derived rule and execution cases. Reproduce the fixed unoptimized baseline, inspect real trade/order/state outcomes with costs and risk, and record the qualification path and its limits. Preserve proof, budgets, lineage, trial ledger, and holdout boundaries. A new behavior or untested context must earn affected evidence again. Deliver a versioned base only on pass; otherwise return with a specific unresolved issue. No Phase 4 tuning, release, or order submission.

Deliver an approval/identity record, method eval and rubric outputs, rule cards and parent links, cumulative ledger, measurement/signal/economic verdicts, reproducible commands and saved development results, and the frozen Phase 3 base if any. Use existing repo evidence locations and schemas; don't introduce infrastructure solely to track these fields.
