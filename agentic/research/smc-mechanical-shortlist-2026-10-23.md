# SMC-like mechanical candidates: research shortlist

Research date: 2026-10-23. Status: candidate recommendation awaiting user judgment. This is research evidence, not a phase-operator handoff, implementation, backtest or approved workflow change. No phase/operator/strategy files were changed for this research.

The user requests that Phase 1 research existing concepts, studies and inspectable implementations before selecting candidates, with a prospective Phase 2 comparison of three coherent strategies. The immediate deliverable is the shortlist; drafting or applying workflow changes requires the later user checkpoint.

## Selection and limits

Rank by SMC fit, distinct trade hypothesis, explicit information timing, inspectability and feasibility for BTCUSDT perpetual 5m/15m research. This is not a ranking of expected profit. No independently verified BTC 5m/15m net edge was established for these three. Prior rejected BTC liquidity prototypes remain development exposure; new labels cannot erase those failures. Published third-party models/backtests also count as selection exposure. A previously reserved window is not automatically pristine for a new source published or developed during that window.

1. **Confirmed structure break followed by an order-block pullback.** Research priority: a structurally defined continuation/pullback candidate, distinct from buying the close after a failed rolling-range break. LuxAlgo SMC and EmreKb MSB/OB expose actual structure/block definitions. They are indicators, not complete broker strategies; choose one authoritative source and explicitly settle block selection, first eligible retest, expiry, stop, target and concurrency before admission to Phase 2. Do not mix the sources silently or trade blocks at their historical anchor before they become known. Start with 5m/15m as hypotheses, not a proven timeframe preference.
2. **Silver Bullet AM session reversal with a FVG retracement entry.** Research priority: an established time-bounded model rather than an unrestricted all-day sweep filter. The inspected Python NQ variant references the 09:00-10:00 New York range, seeks a sweep and displacement during 10:00-11:00, and has FVG/OB/breaker alternatives. A candidate must freeze one entry model rather than cherry-pick branches. The official LuxAlgo indicator documents session FVG retests and recommends chart timeframes below 15 minutes, so 5m is a better source-compatible initial execution hypothesis than forcing it onto 15m. NQ 1m results do not transfer to BTC 5m; New York timezone/DST, market transfer, costs and fills require validation.
3. **Age-qualified Turtle Soup +1 failed breakout.** Research priority: the simplest independently sourced failed-breakout benchmark, with explicit extreme age and delayed reclaim. MarkitTick publishes a 20-prior-bar channel, minimum extreme age four bars, a close outside followed by the next bar reclaiming that original level, confirmed-close signals and defined ATR stop/R targets. This is a published intraday adaptation, not a claim to reproduce the original 20-day book system. The indicator replaces its latest displayed plan on a new signal; that is not permission to erase an open portfolio trade. Execution and exit-allocation policy need explicit decisions.

The three hypotheses are continuation after a structural break, time-localized session reversal, and age-qualified failed-breakout reversal. Previous reclaim/confirmation/FVG prototypes were variants of one bespoke family, not this proposed three-model competition.

## Research evidence

[Deprez and Frommel (2024)](https://biblio.ugent.be/publication/01HY3C3S169G1N6QNYR55NZMFB) study technical rules in Bitcoin with costs, multiple-hypothesis selection and out-of-sample portfolios; their abstract reports positive risk/return findings for selected simple rules. This supports disciplined systematic research, not these SMC candidates specifically. Full paper was not audited here.

[Osler (New York Fed, 2002)](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr150.html) finds evidence of stop-loss-driven FX price cascades. This supports investigating both continuation and reversal hypotheses rather than assuming every sweep reverses. It is FX evidence, not BTC profitability or proof of institutional intent behind a candle.
